I started performing at age 3, and went on to play YOUNG SIMBA in The Lion King, the world's #1 musical. I debuted in South Korea with HORI7ON as the YOUNGEST MALE K-POP IDOL EVER TO DEBUT.

Music is my passion. I love writing songs in Tagalog and English, and I aspire to be the best artist I can be.

My Story

  • Before the stage and the screen, I was already used to being on camera. From 2013 to 2018 I worked with household names in food, toys and real estate, and those years built the confidence and ease in front of the lens that carried into everything that came after.

    Brands I worked with

    Nestle UnitedNestle Bear BrandJack 'n Jill KnotsMagnolia Cheese Milky WhiteToy KingdomCarmona Real EstateMoose Gear

  • My first time performing on TV. Stepping out as Mini Bon Jovi on It's Showtime introduced me to a big audience at a young age, and it was the moment I knew performing was what I wanted to do.

    [ YouTube video ]

  • My first competition win. Entering SM Supermalls' SM Little Stars, I was named Boy Grand Winner, a title that proved I could stand out on a big stage and gave me the push to keep going.

    [ Thumbnail ] ArticleRead the article↗

  • I was named Grand Winner of Cartoon Network Rockstar, and the prize was a real opportunity: they flew me to Singapore to work on a project. There I performed as a voice actor in Cha-Ching Money Smart Kids by Prudence Foundation and Pru Life UK, my first time taking my talent abroad.

    [ Thumbnail ] ArticleRead the article↗

  • I made my film debut in My 2 Mommies, and in a lead role. Carrying a story on screen for the first time showed me, and audiences, that my range goes well beyond singing and the stage.

    [ Thumbnail ] ArticleRead the article↗

  • I played Young Simba in Disney's The Lion King, the world's #1 musical, on an Asian tour through 5 countries and regions and 7 cities. It is the biggest role in the show with the longest character runtime, and I played the role for two full years.

    [ Thumbnail ] ArticleRead the article↗

  • At just 13, I was the youngest contestant on Dream Maker. Thousands of people auditioned to be part of the program, and just over 100 were called back for a few weeks of training. The show started with 62 contestants, and I was one of the 7 who won, which opened the door to my K-pop journey.

    [ YouTube video ]

  • Because I won Dream Maker, the winners were put together as a group and we flew to South Korea for rigorous training, and in July 2023 I debuted with the global boy group HORI7ON as the youngest male K-pop idol ever to debut. Together we made history as the first all-Filipino group to perform on Korean music shows, and the first artist ever to perform a Filipino song on them, all while releasing our album Friend-Ship.

    [ YouTube video ]

  • Right now I'm working and striving toward the next chapter of my career. In my free time I'm working on my music, and I'm seizing every opportunity that unfolds along the way.
